# Storyline choice

Five starting devices considered: one porter carries the story; rain changes a route; a rumor meets a ledger; a rewind splits the afternoon; a full day passes through the same town.

**Selected — A town keeps going.** A flight across actual recorded world states, with one condition changed at a time and the real crossing as the visual spine. The first two seconds already contain walking chibi residents, a recognizable bakery and the river. Rain → finite delivery → real work → inspect a claim → rewind/fork → night and dawn. The look comes directly from the current pastel pixel assets; rounded Nunito text stays large and still while read. Deliberately silent, as authorized; no voice or purchased music. Selected autonomously because an MP4 was explicitly requested.

**Alternative — Follow one loaf.** Object-to-object match cuts follow grain, a pail, oven work and a loaf reaching a resident. A tighter production-chain explanation, but it gives less time to evidence and branching.

**Alternative — The note was wrong.** Start with an actual anonymous note, then follow Wren to a real inspection and compare the receipt with the ledger. A stronger information story, but too narrow for this broader prototype demonstration.
